# Cafe Sales Dashboard


## Project Overview
This project analyzes 2023 cafe sales data to understand sales performance, customer transactions, and product popularity. The dashboard highlights key trends across products, months, payment methods, and locations to support better business decisions.

---

## Dataset
- **Description:** The dataset contains cafe sales transactions from 2023, including product details, sales dates, quantities sold, transaction values, payment methods, and store locations.Some records contain missing or unknown values.
- **Number of Rows:** 10,000
- **Number of Columns:** 8
  
---

## Tools Used
- **Microsoft Excel**
  - Data Cleaning and Preparation: Checking the raw dataset for missing values, Unknown entries, duplicates, and incorrect data formats before analysis.
  - PivotTables and PivotCharts: Summarizing sales data and creating charts to compare product performance, monthly revenue, and daily sales.
  - KPI Calculation: Calculating key metrics such as total revenue, total transactions, and total items sold.
  - Dashboard Design with Slicers: Building an interactive Excel dashboard with filters that allow users to explore sales data by different categories.

## Insight
- Total revenue reached 80,450 across 9,011
transactions, with 27,229 items sold throughout 2023.
- Coffee was the top selling menu, while Salad
contributed the most to total revenue.
- October recorded the highest revenue, while February
had the lowest.
- Sunday had the highest revenue, while Wednesday
recorded the lowest. Overall, sales across the week were
relatively stable.
- Several records contain missing or unknown
information, including 2,870 transactions with an unknown
payment method, 3,577 with an unknown location, and
418 transactions without a date.

--

## Recomendation
- Maintain the quality of popular menu and test meal
bundles to encourage customer to spend more.
- Improve low performing months by introduce targeted
promotions to increase revenue.
- Test Sunday promotions and track whether they
increase both transaction volume and revenue.
